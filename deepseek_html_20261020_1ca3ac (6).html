How to Play

Goal: Poachers attack from all around. Tranquilize them before they grab a pangolin!

  • 🛡️ Protect: Each pangolin has 3 HP. If ANY pangolin's HP reaches 0, game over!
  • ⌨️ Move: W / A / S / D
  • 🖱️ Look & Shoot: Move mouse / drag screen · Left Click to shoot
  • 🦘 Jump: Press Space · 🛷 Slide: Press C
  • 🎁 Collect: Press G near glowing crates
  • 🛒 Shop: Press E. Medkits heal ALL pangolins by 1 HP!
  • 🔍 Zoom: Use Mouse Wheel or Pinch screen to see your character!
  • 🚀 Artillery: RPG explodes and puts groups of poachers to sleep!
